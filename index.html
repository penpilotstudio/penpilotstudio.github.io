FAQ

Common questions

Is it real handwriting or a handwriting font?

Real handwriting. Every page is written by hand with a pen on paper – no fonts and no printouts.

Do you make practical files and lab records?

Yes. Send photos of your experiment list, observations and readings, plus a sample page if your college follows a fixed format. We write the record with index, tables and labelled diagrams.

Do I need to buy the file, register or pens?

No. Pens are included in the ₹20 per page price. We buy and arrange the register – or the file and its sheets – ourselves and charge only their actual cost. You only send the content.

Can you deliver to my home, hostel or college?

Yes. We deliver wherever you tell us – your home, hostel or college, anywhere in India. The delivery charge depends on the location and is added to your quote.

How fast can you deliver?

It depends on the page count and diagrams. Tell us your deadline and we'll confirm the delivery date before you pay.

How is the price decided?

Writing costs ₹20 per page, pen included. Only the register – or the file and its sheets – is charged extra, at actual cost with no margin. Diagrams, urgent deadlines and delivery location can change the total – we confirm the final price on WhatsApp before you pay.

How do I pay?

Once you accept the quote, you make the payment and your order is confirmed. Asking for a quote is completely free.

How will I receive my work?

Physical pages are delivered to your home, hostel or college. If you need it urgently, we can also send scanned copies on WhatsApp.

What if I need changes?

Tell us on WhatsApp. If something doesn't match the instructions you shared, we'll correct it.

Is my information safe?

Your photos and details are used only to complete and deliver your order. We do not sell or publish them.

Do you solve questions or write assignments?

No. We do not solve questions or create original academic work. We only rewrite, format and present content that you provide. You are responsible for making sure your use of our service follows the rules of your school, college or university.
